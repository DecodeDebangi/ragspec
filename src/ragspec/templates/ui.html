✓ragspecRAG Evaluator

Automated tests for chatbots that answer from your documents

A RAG chatbot searches your documents, then writes an answer from what it finds. Small changes can quietly break it. ragspec catches that by running the same test questions before and after a change.

  1. Did it find the right documents?Compares the search results with the documents known to hold the answer.
  2. Does the answer stick to them?An AI judge checks every fact against the documents and its citations.
  3. Did the change make it worse?Compares before and after, and fails the check if the drop is real.

    Each comparison calls your LLM API, up to 4 requests.
